Calibration & Guide

Learn standard poses and calibrate hand positions
1. Bounding Box Calibration Position your hand in the center of your web camera. The mapper maps coordinates inside the active center bounding box.
2. Pinch-to-Click Bring your index finger and thumb together to click. Keep them pinched and move your hand to drag.
3. Two-Finger Scroll Raise both index and middle fingers side-by-side. Move your hand up and down to scroll vertically.
